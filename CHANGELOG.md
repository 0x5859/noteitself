# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Notebooks with lined, grid, dotted or blank pages, and an infinite canvas.
- Low-latency Apple Pencil ink on a Metal renderer: pen, pencil, brush, marker
  and highlighter, with per-tool size, paper feel and smoothing, and natural
  ink for the pen, pencil and brush.
- Precise and whole-stroke eraser; Pencil double-tap and squeeze actions;
  palm-safe touch handling; two- and three-finger tap for undo and redo.
- Layers, and image layers for tracing.
- PDF import and annotation; export to vector PDF.
- Pixel-art editor with layers, fill, eyedropper, PNG and PDF export, and a
  free canvas size of up to 256 × 256 pixels.
- Notes are plain `.noteitself` packages in the app's Documents folder,
  visible in the Files app.
